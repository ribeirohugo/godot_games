# DEAD SECTOR

## FPS Campaign Story & Level Design

**Genre:** First-person shooter  
**Campaign:** 12 missions  
**Style:** Fast combat, abandoned military facilities, industrial environments, urban warfare, underground complexes  
**Story:** Military conspiracy + biological disaster + experimental technology

---

# THE STORY

## The Incident

In 2047, an experimental energy facility called **Sector 17** suffers a catastrophic accident.

Sector 17 isn't a normal power plant. It is a classified military research installation built beneath an abandoned industrial city.

The facility's purpose is **Project HELIX** — an experimental technology capable of rapidly repairing damaged biological tissue.

Something goes wrong during an experiment.

Within minutes, everyone inside the facility dies.

The surrounding region is sealed.

The government officially declares the area a **Dead Sector**.

No one is allowed inside.

For six months, nothing happens.

Then, one night, an emergency transmission comes from inside the sector:

> "They're still alive."

A military reconnaissance team is sent in.

It disappears.

You play as **Captain Jack Mercer**, a member of the government's **Black Division**, an elite unit used for operations that officially never happened.

Your team is sent into the Dead Sector.

### Your Orders

1. Find the missing team.
2. Determine what happened.
3. Recover Project HELIX.
4. If recovery isn't possible: **destroy the facility.**

# CAMPAIGN

## LEVEL 01 — THE DROP

### Location

The outskirts of the Dead Sector.

A ruined highway leading toward the quarantine zone.

### Objective

Reach the quarantine checkpoint and establish contact with the reconnaissance team.

### Map

The opening level is relatively linear.

You begin in a military transport helicopter.

The helicopter approaches the sector at night.

Below you:

- Abandoned highways
- Destroyed cars
- Burning buildings
- Military checkpoints
- Empty streets

Then the helicopter is hit.

You crash outside the quarantine perimeter.

### Gameplay

You start with a basic assault rifle and pistol.

The first enemies are **rogue security soldiers**.

They're not monsters yet.

They're people wearing military equipment.

This establishes the game's tone.

You reach the checkpoint and discover that the soldiers defending it have been dead for weeks.

Then you hear gunfire from inside.

The gates suddenly open.

Something runs through them.

You never get a good look at it.

**End of level.**

---

## LEVEL 02 — QUARANTINE

### Location

Military quarantine zone.

### Objective

Reach Checkpoint Zero and recover the reconnaissance team's equipment.

### Map

This level introduces the larger outdoor environments.

You move through:

- Abandoned military vehicles
- Checkpoints
- Temporary barracks
- Ammunition depots
- Medical tents
- Observation towers
- Concrete barriers

The sector is completely abandoned.

But the electricity is still working.

That's the problem.

Some buildings have power.

Others don't.

The player has to activate generators to open certain gates.

### Map Mechanic

Some areas are inaccessible until you restore power.

Example:

`Generator → Security Building → Gate → New Area`

But restoring power also activates automated defenses.

So opening the way forward makes the level more dangerous.

### Enemies

- Rogue soldiers
- Security drones
- Automated turrets

At the end, you find the reconnaissance team's vehicle.

Everyone is dead.

Their body cameras are missing.

---

## LEVEL 03 — GHOST TOWN

### Location

The abandoned city surrounding Sector 17.

### Objective

Cross the city and reach the underground entrance to the research facility.

This is the first major **urban FPS level**.

You fight through:

- Apartments
- Shops
- Parking garages
- Alleys
- Offices
- Subway entrances

The city hasn't been destroyed by war.

It was **evacuated**.

Cars are still parked everywhere.

Restaurants still have food inside.

TVs are still running.

The unsettling part is how recently everyone disappeared.

### Level Gimmick

Some buildings are connected internally.

Instead of having one obvious route, the player can move through:

`Street → Shop → Apartment → Rooftop → Adjacent Building`

This gives the level a slightly more open feel without requiring an enormous open world.

### Story Discovery

You find civilians hiding inside an apartment.

They're dead.

But their television is still playing an emergency broadcast:

> "Remain indoors. Military assistance is on the way."

The broadcast is six months old.

---

## LEVEL 04 — THE NEST

### Location

Underground subway system.

### Objective

Use the subway tunnels to reach Sector 17.

This is where the game becomes noticeably darker.

The subway is almost completely unpowered.

You need to manually activate electrical substations.

### Map

The subway has collapsed sections.

The player has to navigate:

- Maintenance tunnels
- Flooded platforms
- Train carriages
- Ventilation systems
- Abandoned stations

Some tunnels are flooded.

Water makes movement slower and disables certain electrical equipment.

### New Enemy — The Infected

These were humans exposed to HELIX.

They're fast and aggressive.

They don't use weapons.

This is the first time the player realizes:

> **Something biological happened here.**

---

## LEVEL 05 — SECTOR 17

### Location

The main military research facility.

This is the first major classic FPS facility level.

Huge industrial corridors.

Laboratories.

Security checkpoints.

Armories.

Server rooms.

Medical facilities.

### Objective

Find the missing reconnaissance team.

The facility is still partially operational.

Doors open automatically.

Lights turn on as you approach.

Computer terminals activate.

But nobody is controlling them.

### Level Structure

RESEARCH  
↓  
CONTAINMENT  
↓  
CORE

The player initially can't access the Core.

You need three security cards.

That creates a natural loop through the facility.

---

## LEVEL 06 — CONTAINMENT

### Location

HELIX biological containment facility.

This is the game's horror-heavy level.

You discover hundreds of containment chambers.

Most are empty.

Some aren't.

### Map Mechanic

Containment doors can fail.

Sometimes opening one room causes another section to unlock.

You can hear things moving behind sealed doors.

But you don't immediately know what.

### Enemy — HELIX Mutants

They're former soldiers and scientists.

Their bodies have been heavily altered by the experimental technology.

They're much stronger than the normal infected.

### Major Discovery

You find the missing reconnaissance team.

They're alive.

But they're imprisoned inside containment chambers.

They tell you:

> "We weren't sent here to investigate."

> "We were sent here to retrieve something."

The military already knew what was inside.

---

## LEVEL 07 — BLACK SITE

### Location

A secret military facility beneath Sector 17.

This wasn't on any official map.

The facility is much newer than Sector 17.

Someone built it **after the original disaster**.

### Objective

Find out who has been operating inside the Dead Sector.

The level is more militarized:

- Armories
- Command rooms
- Server farms
- Weapons laboratories
- Training areas
- Underground hangars

You begin fighting **Black Division soldiers**.

They're human.

And they're ordered to kill you.

### Story Twist

You discover recordings showing your own commander entering Sector 17 six months earlier.

The military didn't lose control of the Dead Sector.

**They created it.**

---

## LEVEL 08 — THE FACILITY

### Location

The HELIX core.

This is the largest map in the campaign.

It's essentially an enormous underground industrial complex.

Think:

**Half military base + half power plant + half laboratory.**

### Map Design

Multiple vertical layers:

SURFACE  
↓  
INDUSTRIAL LEVEL  
↓  
RESEARCH LEVEL  
↓  
CONTAINMENT  
↓  
HELIX CORE

The player repeatedly moves between these layers.

Elevators, maintenance shafts and industrial platforms connect them.

### Environmental Hazards

The facility is collapsing.

You encounter:

- Steam explosions
- Electrical arcs
- Toxic gas
- Fires
- Collapsing floors
- Malfunctioning machinery

The environment itself becomes part of the combat.

---

## LEVEL 09 — BREACH

Everything goes wrong.

The HELIX containment system fails.

The entire facility goes into emergency lockdown.

Every enemy type is now loose.

### Gameplay

This is the game's big chaotic level.

You aren't carefully exploring anymore.

You're trying to survive.

Doors slam shut behind you.

Alarm lights flash.

Enemies pour into previously cleared areas.

You have to fight your way back toward the surface.

### Major Set Piece

A huge containment chamber breaks apart.

Something enormous escapes.

You don't fight it.

**You run.**

The creature chases you through the collapsing facility.

You barely escape.

---

## LEVEL 10 — DEAD CITY

### Location

The city outside Sector 17.

The entire quarantine zone has become infected.

The military has arrived.

Their solution:

**Bomb the city.**

You have approximately 30 minutes before the strike.

### Objective

Reach the extraction point.

This becomes the game's largest outdoor combat map.

You fight through:

- Streets
- Military barricades
- Destroyed buildings
- Burning vehicles
- Evacuation zones
- Rooftops

Military forces are fighting the infected.

You are caught between both sides.

---

## LEVEL 11 — THE TRUTH

You reach the extraction facility.

But your commander betrays you.

He reveals the actual purpose of HELIX.

It wasn't designed to cure disease.

It was designed to create **self-repairing soldiers**.

The infected aren't failed experiments.

They're the **first generation**.

The military wants the technology because it works.

### Final Objective

Destroy the HELIX core before the military extracts it.

You return underground.

The facility is now almost completely destroyed.

---

# LEVEL 12 — DEAD SECTOR

## Final Level

You return to the HELIX chamber.

The entire facility is collapsing.

The final enemy is the first successful HELIX soldier.

Not a giant monster.

A **human-sized super-soldier**.

Extremely fast.

Extremely strong.

Capable of regenerating.

---

# FINAL BOSS — THE FIRST

The fight takes place inside the HELIX reactor chamber.

The arena changes throughout the fight.

### Phase 1 — Normal Combat

The player fights the boss in the main reactor arena.

### Phase 2 — Reactor Failure

The reactor begins exploding.

Parts of the arena disappear.

The player has to constantly reposition.

### Phase 3 — Regeneration

The boss begins regenerating.

You have to destroy the reactor's containment systems to prevent regeneration.

### Phase 4 — Collapse

Both you and the boss are fighting while the entire facility collapses.

Eventually you destroy HELIX.

---

# ENDING

You escape the facility as Sector 17 collapses.

From a helicopter, you look back at the Dead Sector.

The entire region is burning.

Your commander tells you:

> "Nobody will ever know what happened here."

Then your radio receives a transmission.

A military satellite has detected a HELIX signal.

Not from Sector 17.

**From somewhere else.**

The screen cuts to black.

# DEAD SECTOR

**MISSION COMPLETE**

Then:

SIGNAL DETECTED  
LOCATION: CLASSIFIED

This leaves the door open for **Dead Sector 2** without requiring the first game to have a cliffhanger that feels unfinished.
