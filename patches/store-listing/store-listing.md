## English — Patches

### Short description (recommended, up to 1,000 characters; keep under 270 for best display)

Cover the grid with rectangular patches, each one matching its clue — the number is its area, the shape says square, wide or tall. Pick Easy, Medium or Hard, no daily limit.

(173 characters)

### Description (required, up to 10,000 characters)

Patches is a grid-filling logic puzzle: cover the whole board with non-overlapping rectangular patches, one for every clue. Each clue gives you the patch's exact area and its shape — square, wide or tall — and every puzzle has exactly one solution.

Drag across the grid to draw a patch; drawing over an existing patch replaces it, and tapping a patch removes it. Once the board is full, every clue must sit under exactly one rectangle of the right size and shape, with no gaps and no overlaps.

Pick Easy, Medium or Hard instead of waiting for a daily puzzle — no daily limit, play as many boards as you want. Stuck? Undo your last patch, clear the board, or ask for a hint.

Track your progress in Statistics: puzzles played and solved, current and best streaks, and best times and hint-free solves for every difficulty. Play with the mouse, the keyboard, or an Xbox controller, in English, Portuguese, Spanish, French or German.

(931 characters)

### Product features (200 characters per feature, 20 max — Store adds bullets automatically, do not add your own)

1. Cover the grid with rectangular patches, each matching its clue's exact area and shape
2. Drag to draw a patch, drag over one to replace it, or tap a patch to remove it
3. Three difficulty levels, freshly generated each time — no daily puzzle, play whenever you like
4. Undo, clear the board, and hints that point to your next patch or flag a mistake
