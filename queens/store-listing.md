## English — Queens

### Short description (recommended, up to 1,000 characters; keep under 270 for best display)

Place one queen in every row, column and colored region — no two queens touching, not even diagonally. Pick Easy, Medium or Hard and play as many boards as you like, no daily limit.

(181 characters)

### Description (required, up to 10,000 characters)

Queens is a logic puzzle: place exactly one queen in every row, every column and every colored region of the board, with no two queens touching — not even diagonally. Every puzzle has exactly one solution, and another is ready the moment you finish.

Tap a cell to cycle it through empty, ×, and queen, or drag across cells to mark several × at once. Right-click (or the Y button on a controller) places or removes a queen directly, and an auto-× option crosses out cells a placed queen already rules out.

Pick Easy, Medium or Hard instead of waiting for a daily puzzle — no daily limit, play as many boards as you want. Stuck? Undo your last move, clear the board, or ask for a hint.

Track your progress in Statistics: puzzles played and solved, current and best streaks, and best times and hint-free solves for every difficulty. Play with the mouse, the keyboard, or an Xbox controller, in English, Portuguese, Spanish, French or German.

Free, ad-free, no in-app purchases.

(978 characters)

1. One queen in every row, column and colored region — no two queens touching, even diagonally

3. Three difficulty levels, freshly generated each time — no daily puzzle, play whenever you like
4. Undo, clear the board, hints, and an auto-× option that crosses out ruled-out cells
5. Free, ad-free, no in-app purchases
